# Hearthborne

HEARTHBORNE
Being part of a hearthborne community means you come
from humble origins, having lived in a modest village or quaint
countryside. These communities are often small groups that
live in resource-rich, peaceful areas that allow families and
neighbors the time to develop close-knit relationships rather
than compete over scarce supplies. Hearthborne communities
are largely agrarian and pastoral, focusing the layout of their
villages around their plants and animals. The town centers
typically feature a collection of locally owned shops with
skilled artisans such as blacksmiths, cobblers, alchemists,
and the like. Sometimes, hearthborne communities have
distant but powerful rulers who own the land the villagers
work and live on—in those cases, resources are controlled
by the wealthy even though the labor is performed by locals,
and workers might struggle to afford the basics they need
to survive. While some hearthborne individuals can be
mistrustful of outsiders, most are known for being welcoming,
peaceful, and hospitable.
Hearthborne are often careful, generous, insular, loquacious,
smiley, and traditional.
