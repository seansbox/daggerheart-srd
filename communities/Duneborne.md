# Duneborne

DUNEBORNE
Being part of a duneborne community means you’ve made
a home among the shifting sands and arid climate of the
desert. Each duneborne enclave develops specific methods
of protecting themselves from the blazing sun, moving across
the scorching sands, and acquiring and storing precious
resources. Some duneborne travel their desert homes,
maneuvering through the dunes herding livestock, while
others cluster around oases, collaborating with one another
to maintain and share a rare area of abundant resources.
Duneborne settlements are known for architecture that
captures wind to cool their homes and irrigation systems that
tap into underground reservoirs and tend to crops even amidst
the harshest conditions. Many duneborne put added emphasis
on familial bonds and the need for everyone to contribute to
the well-being of the collective.
Duneborne are often adaptable, centered, collaborative, devoted,
cheerful, and observant.
COMMUNITY FEATURE
