# Frostborne

FROSTBORNE
Being part of a frostborne community means you come from
a place of snow and ice. Forged in bitter and unrelenting
cold, frostborne communities are accustomed to surviving
in the harshest conditions. The glaciers and tundras they call
home usually lack wood, so these collectives use more readily
available materials such as ice, stone, bone, and animal pelts
to make shelter and conserve heat. They are adept at ensuring
their supplies go a long way and that all their people have
access to the essentials needed to survive. As part of their
systems of survival, many frostborne closely track weather
patterns, the rising and setting of the sun, and the needs of
local flora and fauna. Because of the howling wind and biting
snow, some frostborne communities develop nonverbal
means of communication so they can cover their faces for
safety during dangerous weather.
Frostborne are often cunning, gregarious, intrepid, purposeful,
quiet, and warm.
