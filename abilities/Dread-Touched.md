# Dread-Touched

**_Level 7_** _Dread Ability._ **_Recall Cost_** _2._

When 4 or more of the domain cards in your loadout are from
the Dread domain, gain the following benefits:
 ◦ When you succeed with Fear, you can mark 2 Stress  to
prevent the GM from gaining a Fear.
 ◦ Once per rest when making an action roll, you can gain a
bonus to the roll equal to the number of Fear in the GM’s
pool.
