# Siphon Essence

**_Level 2_** _Dread Spell._ **_Recall Cost_** _1._

Make a Spellcast Roll against a target within Very Close range.
Once per long rest on a success, the target takes d12+4  magic
damage using your Proficiency. On a success with Fear, you
gain a +1 bonus to your Proficiency for this attack.
You clear a number of Hit Points equal to the number of Hit
Points the target marked from this attack.
