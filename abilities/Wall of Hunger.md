# Wall of Hunger

**_Level 7_** _Dread Spell._ **_Recall Cost_** _2._

Make a Spellcast Roll (10) . On a success, you can spend
a Hope to create a visible wall of writhing necrotic energy
between two points within Far range. The wall lasts until you
mark a Hit Point or cast this spell again. A creature inside the
wall when it appears or that passes through it must mark 2
Stress.
