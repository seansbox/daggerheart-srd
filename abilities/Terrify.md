# Terrify

**_Level 3_** _Dread Spell._ **_Recall Cost_** _1._

Make a Spellcast Roll against a target within Close range. On
a success, the target marks 1d4  Stress, and you can make the
target flee one range away from you (such as Very Close to
Close or Close to Far). On a success with Fear, the target also
becomes temporarily Vulnerable.
