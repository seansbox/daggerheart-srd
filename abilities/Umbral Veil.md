# Umbral Veil

**_Level 1_** _Dread Spell._ **_Recall Cost_** _1._

Once per rest, you can mark a Stress to encase yourself in
shadowy energy. When you do, place a number of tokens on
this card equal to the number of Fear in the GM’s pool. After an
attack roll is made against you, you can spend any number of
tokens to give the result a −1 penalty per token spent.
At the end of the scene, clear all unspent tokens.
