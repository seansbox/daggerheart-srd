# Darkfire

**_Level 6_** _Dread Spell._ **_Recall Cost_** _2._

Once per scene,  spend any number of Hope to target an equal
number of adversaries within Close range. Each target makes
a Reaction Roll (15) . T argets who fail take d8+6  magic damage
using your Spellcast trait as they are engulfed in dark fire.
T argets who succeed take half damage.
